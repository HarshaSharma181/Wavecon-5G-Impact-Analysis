# Wavecon 5G Impact Analysis 📊

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the impact of the **Wavecon Telecom 5G launch** on revenue, customer KPIs, city performance, and plan performance.

The analysis was completed as part of the **Week 2 Codebasics Virtual Internship** with AtliQ Technologies, with a focus on turning telecom data into actionable business insights using **Power BI**.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis answers five key business questions:

1. What was the impact of the 5G launch on revenue?
2. Which customer KPI is underperforming after the 5G launch?
3. Which plans are performing well and which are underperforming?
4. Which plan was most affected by the 5G launch, and should it be continued or discontinued?
5. Which plans were discontinued after the launch and why?

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Data visualization, KPI analysis, dashboard development and insights
* **Microsoft PowerPoint** – Business storytelling and presentation

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 💰 Revenue Impact

* Revenue before 5G: **₹16.0B**
* Revenue after 5G: **₹15.9B**
* Overall change: **-0.5%**
* ARPU increased by **11%**, helping offset the decline in revenue.

Revenue remained almost stable, but the growth in ARPU was accompanied by a decline in the active customer base.

### 👥 Customer KPI Performance

| KPI                      | Before 5G | After 5G |  Change |
| ------------------------ | --------: | -------: | ------: |
| Total Active Users (TAU) |     84.4M |    77.4M |  -8.28% |
| Unsubscribed Users       |      5.6M |     7.0M | +23.50% |

The major concern is **customer retention**, as active users declined while unsubscribed users increased.

### 🏙️ City Performance

**Mumbai** remained the top revenue-generating city:

* Before 5G: **₹2.444B**
* After 5G: **₹2.452B**

The major revenue-generating cities remained relatively stable, although **Kolkata overtook Delhi for the #2 position** after the 5G launch.

### 📦 Plan Performance

#### Plans Performing Well

* **p1 – Smart Recharge Pack:** ₹1.8B → ₹2.4B (**+33%**)
* **p11 – Ultra Fast Mega Pack:** ₹1.9B revenue in its first four months
* **p12 / p13 – New 5G combos:** ₹1.2B and ₹0.31B respectively

#### Underperforming Plans

* **p7 – 25GB 3G/4G Combo:** ₹582M → ₹156M (**-73%**)
* **p5 – ₹99 Talktime Combo:** ₹1.0B → ₹652M (**-35%**)
* **p6 – Xstream 15GB Pack:** ₹749M → ₹495M (**-34%**)

### ⚠️ Most Affected Plan

**p7 – 25GB 3G/4G Combo**

* Before 5G: **₹582.4M**
* After 5G: **₹155.6M**
* Revenue decline: **73%**

The analysis recommends **discontinuing p7** and migrating remaining subscribers toward stronger plans such as **p11 or p1**.

### 🗑️ Discontinued Plans

The following plans were discontinued after the 5G launch:

* **p8 – Daily Saviour**
* **p9 – Combo TopUp**
* **p10 – Big Combo Pack**

These were short-validity, low-data plans designed for the pre-5G environment. They were replaced by the newer 5G combo family (**p11–p13**) offering longer validity and stronger data.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Prioritize customer retention** through targeted win-back campaigns.
2. **Retire p7** and migrate its subscribers to stronger plans such as p11 or p1.
3. **Increase marketing focus on successful 5G plans**, especially p1 and p11–p13.
4. **Track ARPU and TAU together** every month so ARPU growth does not hide an unsustainable decline in active users.
